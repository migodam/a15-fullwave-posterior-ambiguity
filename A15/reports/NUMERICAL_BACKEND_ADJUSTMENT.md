# Numerical backend adjustment before discovery / confirmation
The approved VIE grid study at theta=(.083,.7,.023), k=6, radius=.12, epsilon=12 showed single-cylinder relative response errors of 6.07%, 2.67%, 1.43%, .732%, .416% at n=32/48/64/96/128. This is inadequate for fine 1% noise inference; it is a numerical limitation, not evidence against the physical mechanism.
Because the already approved objects are homogeneous circles, an independent exact multiple-cylinder Helmholtz expansion was added. It solves all inter-body multiple scattering by a coupled outgoing/regular cylindrical-wave system, not by a two-mode approximation. Its order is verified independently, power balance checked, and the original VIE retained for spatial convergence/cross-check. The research prior, acquisition budget, noise and success criteria are unchanged. No validation/test data were opened.
Current coordinates are mass-normalized internal contrast-current coefficients; coordinate metric changes with physical parameters, so tangent differentiation includes the full assembled matrix, RHS and readout derivatives. Modes from the two-mode diagnostic are distinct from this converged full-wave engine.
